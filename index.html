07Questions

Before you start.

The questions founders ask me most, answered straight.

1How much does a website cost?

It depends on how many pages and sections you need. After our first call I send one fixed price for the whole project, so there are no surprises later.

2How long does it take?

It depends on the size of the site. You get a timeline together with the price, before any work starts.

3Do you build it too, or only design it?

Both. I design every page in Figma, and once you're happy I build the site so it's ready to launch.

4How do we work across time zones?

I'm in Pakistan and work with founders abroad. We meet on calls, and I share progress in Figma so you can comment whenever suits you.

5Can you redesign the site we already have?

Yes. I start with what your current site gets wrong for buyers, keep what works, and rebuild the rest.

6What do you need from me?

Your product, who it's for, and 30 minutes on a call. A logo and content help, but we can sort those out together.
